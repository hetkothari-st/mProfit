Before you write

When can I start using EveryPaisa?

We're inviting people in small groups. Request early access and we'll email you when your place is ready.

Can my CA use it for several clients?

Yes. The Pro / Adviser plan gives a professional workspace with unlimited client books, double-entry accounting and Tally export.

Is there a mobile app?

EveryPaisa works fully in your phone's browser and can be added to your home screen. Native apps are in progress. A Windows desktop app is available.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. Settings has a delete option that removes your account and records. Download any reports you want to keep first.
