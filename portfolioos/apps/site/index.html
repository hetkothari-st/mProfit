How it works

Your inbox already has the data. We read it, you approve it.

  1. Pick the senders you trust

    Connect Gmail with read-only access and choose your bank, broker and registrar. We only ever search mail from those addresses, never the rest of your inbox.

  2. Each alert becomes an entry

    Amount, date, instrument and account are pulled out of every alert, SIP debit and contract note. Personal identifiers are stripped before anything is parsed.

  3. You approve, once

    New entries wait in a review queue. After five clean entries from a sender, you can let that sender file straight into the ledger.

  4. The ledger adds up

    Holdings, gains, XIRR and net worth recalculate from the transactions themselves, so the numbers are always traceable back to a source.

    Questions

    Things people ask before they trust us with their money.

    Can EveryPaisa move my money or place trades?

    No. EveryPaisa only reads. It has no way to move money, place orders or change anything at your bank or broker.

    Do you read my whole inbox?

    No. You choose the senders, like your bank's alert address or your broker. We search only mail from those addresses, with read-only access you can revoke from your Google account at any time.

    Which brokers and fund houses are supported?

    Mutual funds come in through your CAS from CAMS and KFintech or MF Central, which covers every AMC. Stocks come from contract notes and tradebooks from major brokers including Zerodha, Upstox, Angel One and Kotak, from your demat CAS, or from the Account Aggregator network. Anything else can be added from Excel or by hand.

    How accurate are the capital gains?

    Gains are computed from your transactions lot by lot, using FIFO, with the 31 January 2018 grandfathering rule and current holding-period rules for equity, debt and hybrid funds. Each figure links back to the buys and sells it came from, so your CA can check it.

    What happens to my data if I leave?

    Every report can be downloaded as PDF or Excel at any time. Deleting your account removes your records from our systems.

    Is this investment advice?

    EveryPaisa is a tracking and reporting tool. Scores, insights and suggestions help you understand your money; they aren't a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
