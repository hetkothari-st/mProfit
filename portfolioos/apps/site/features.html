Getting your data in

Bring in years of history in an afternoon, then let new transactions arrive on their own.

Gmail, read-only

Choose the senders to watch. Alerts and statements from them become ledger entries; nothing else in your inbox is touched.

Sender discovery

A one-time scan suggests which senders in your inbox look financial, so you don't have to remember every bank and broker.

Review queue

Approve, edit or reject each parsed entry. Trust a sender after five clean entries and its alerts file automatically.

CAS statements

Upload the consolidated statement from CAMS and KFintech, or the demat CAS from NSDL and CDSL, to load every holding at once.

MF Central & Account Aggregator

Pull mutual fund, bank and demat data directly, with your consent, through MF Central and the RBI's Account Aggregator network.

Broker files

Contract notes, tradebooks and F&O P&L statements from major brokers, plus EPF passbooks, Excel and CSV.

No duplicates

Import the same statement twice and nothing is added twice. Every entry remembers the file or email it came from.

Failed rows kept, not lost

If one line of a statement can't be read, the rest still import and that line waits for you to fix or enter by hand.

Corporate actions

Splits, bonuses, mergers and dividends are applied to your holdings so quantities and costs stay right.

Net worth & analytics

One page that adds everything up, and the tools to ask better questions of it.

Dashboard

Net worth, allocation, invested amount, today's change and the alerts that need you this week.

Returns that mean something

XIRR for each holding, portfolio and the whole family, plus holding-period returns over 1, 3 and 5 years.

Analytics

Best and worst performers, risk, correlation between your holdings, liabilities and cash flow in one place.

What-if simulator

Move money between asset classes and see how your allocation and expected return would change.

Portfolio score

A health score for your money with specific, plain-language fixes: concentration, idle cash, cover, costs.

Cash activity & forecast

Every inflow and outflow across accounts, and a forecast of the months ahead from your EMIs, SIPs, rent and premiums.

Income

Salary, rent, interest and dividends in one register, ready for your tax return.

Goals

Retirement, a child's education, a home. Link investments to each goal and see if you're on track.

Ask in plain English

"What's my XIRR this year?" or "Am I overweight in IT?" The assistant answers from your own ledger.

Alerts

Maturities, renewals, expiries, rent due, premiums, overdue items. One list, by date.

Multiple portfolios

Keep a trading account, a retirement pot and your parents' money apart, then group them however you like.

Security

Designed on the assumption that this is the most sensitive data you have.

Encryption at rest

PAN, account, folio and policy numbers are encrypted with a key unique to each user.

Per-user isolation

Row-level security in the database stops one person's data being read by another, even if our own code makes a mistake.

Identifiers removed before AI

Names, PAN and account numbers are stripped from every alert before it is parsed.

Two-factor sign-in

Authenticator app codes and backup codes.

Audit log

Sign-ins, exports and views of sensitive details are recorded.

Download & delete

Download any report as PDF or Excel, or delete your account and every record in it.
