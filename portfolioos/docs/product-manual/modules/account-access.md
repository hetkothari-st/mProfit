# Account Access

> Product: EveryPaisa · Generated: 2026-10-23 · Source commit: 9d9025c4
> UI verification: Code-only — not visually verified
> Scope: Whole application

**Account Access** (sidebar item for everyone, route `/settings/professional-access`, page title "Account access") is where you, the account holder, bring in your CA or advisor and decide what they can see, what they can do and until when. It is never plan-gated: "Whoever can reach someone's financial position, the person it belongs to must be able to see it and stop it." The professional's side of the same relationship (the **Client books** workspace) is documented by the advice/CA chapter ([D-SET-004](../dependencies.md#d-set-004)).
