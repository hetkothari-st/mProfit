# Write and generate a will

`W-WILL-001` · Will Maker

**Goal:** Produce a will PDF from your EveryPaisa data.
**Who:** [R-PLUS](../roles.md#r-plus), [R-FAMILY](../roles.md#r-family), [R-PRO](../roles.md#r-pro) on their own account.

| # | You do | The app does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Planning → Will Maker → Create my will** | Wizard opens at **Your details** ([F-WILL-001](../features/F-WILL-001.md)) |
| 2 | Fill **Your details**, **Next** | Saved automatically; blockers listed ([F-WILL-005](../features/F-WILL-005.md)) |
| 3 | **People & roles**: add beneficiaries, choose executor (and guardian if any child is under 18) | [F-WILL-006](../features/F-WILL-006.md) |
| 4 | **Assets & liabilities**: review each item, set ownership | [F-WILL-007](../features/F-WILL-007.md) |
| 5 | **Allocate**: shares to 100%, gifts, survival period | [F-WILL-008](../features/F-WILL-008.md) |
| 6 | **Instructions** (optional), **Review**: fix every cross | **Next** enabled at "Ready to preview" ([F-WILL-010](../features/F-WILL-010.md)) |
| 7 | **Preview**: tick the acknowledgement, **Generate PDF** | "Version N is ready" ([F-WILL-011](../features/F-WILL-011.md)) |

**Success:** Status **Generated**; **Download PDF** available.
**If something goes wrong**
| Symptom / message | Why | How to recover |
|---|---|---|
| "This will was changed in another tab or device…" | Edited elsewhere | **Reload** |
| "Your preview is from an earlier day…" | Date changed | **Reload preview** |
| Foreign/Muslim law notice | Unsupported | Consult an advocate |
