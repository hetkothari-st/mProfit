# Request a CAS from CAMS and KFintech

`W-MF-002` · Mutual Funds

**Goal:** Have both registrars email you a CAS, then import it.
**Who:** [R-FREE](../roles.md#r-free), [R-PLUS](../roles.md#r-plus), [R-FAMILY](../roles.md#r-family), [R-PRO](../roles.md#r-pro).
**Before you start:** Know the email registered on your folios; Gmail connected if you want auto-import ([D-MF-004](../dependencies.md#d-mf-004)).

| # | You do | The app does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Click **Sync via CAMS / KFintech** | Loads your saved PAN |
| 2 | If prompted, **Set PAN now** → type PAN → **Save PAN** | "PAN saved to profile" |
| 3 | Enter **Email**, choose **Period** ([S-MF-004](../settings/mutual-funds.md#s-mf-004)), click **Continue** | Opens both portals; toast "CAMS & KFintech reached" |
| 4 | Type each captcha shown | — |
| 5 | Click **Submit CAS Request** within 5 minutes of step 3 | Shows per-portal OK / failed result |
| 6 | Wait 5–60 minutes for the emails | Gmail import (if configured) or upload the PDF via [W-MF-001](W-MF-001.md) |

**Success:** **CAS request submitted** with "CAMS: OK" and/or "KFintech: OK".
**If something goes wrong**
| Symptom / message | Why | How to recover |
|---|---|---|
| "Failed to reach CAMS or KFintech portals. Try again or use PDF upload." | Portals unreachable | Retry later or use [W-MF-001](W-MF-001.md) |
| "failed — CAMS session expired" | More than 5 minutes passed | Start again |
| "failed — … rejected the captcha" | Wrong captcha | Start again |
| No email | Email or PAN not on the folios | Check with the registrar |
