# Set up a family and keep a grandparent's books

`W-FAM-001` · Family

**Goal:** Create a family, add a relative with no email, and record their holdings.
**Who:** [R-FAMILY](../roles.md#r-family) or [R-PRO](../roles.md#r-pro) (becomes [R-FAMOWNER](../roles.md#r-famowner)); then [R-MANAGER](../roles.md#r-manager).
**Before you start:** FAMILY plan or higher.

| # | You do | The app does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Overview → Family → New family**, type a name, **Create** | Toast "Family created"; you are Owner; Overview tab opens ([F-FAM-002](../features/F-FAM-002.md)) |
| 2 | **Members & sharing → Add member** | "Add a family member" opens on **Add them now** |
| 3 | Enter **Their name**, choose **Related to** you and **As their** (e.g. Father), leave **Who keeps their books** as you, click **Add to family** | Toast "<name> added to the family"; "<name> is on the tree."; a "<first name>'s portfolio" is created ([F-FAM-009](../features/F-FAM-009.md)) |
| 4 | Click **Open <name>'s account** | Toast "Now managing <name>'s account"; dashboard opens with the amber strip "You are managing …" ([F-FAM-024](../features/F-FAM-024.md)) |
| 5 | Add their FDs, insurance etc. on the usual pages | Everything is saved to their account |
| 6 | Click **Back to my account** | Dashboard opens in your own account |

**Success:** The relative appears on the tree, in the sidebar family tree and in "Accounts you manage".
**If something goes wrong**
| Symptom / message | Why | How to recover |
|---|---|---|
| "Family Sharing is locked" | Plan below FAMILY | Upgrade on **Pricing** |
| Seat payment message | 3 included seats used | Pay ₹199 for a seat ([F-FAM-012](../features/F-FAM-012.md)) or cancel an open invitation |
| "You cannot act for this profile." | You are not the keeper or a membership ended | Ask an owner to set you as keeper ([F-FAM-015](../features/F-FAM-015.md)) |
