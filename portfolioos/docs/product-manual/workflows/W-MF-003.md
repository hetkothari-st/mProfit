# Request a CAS through CASParser

`W-MF-003` · Mutual Funds

**Goal:** Get a KFintech-issued CAS (covering CAMS and KFintech funds) by PAN + email, auto-imported from Gmail.
**Who:** [R-FREE](../roles.md#r-free), [R-PLUS](../roles.md#r-plus), [R-FAMILY](../roles.md#r-family), [R-PRO](../roles.md#r-pro).
**Before you start:** Gmail connected ([D-MF-004](../dependencies.md#d-mf-004)) for automatic import.

| # | You do | The app does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Click **Sync MF via CASParser** | Opens **Sync Mutual Funds via PAN + Email**; shows CASParser credits when available |
| 2 | Enter **PAN**, **Email**, choose **Period** ([S-MF-001](../settings/mutual-funds.md#s-mf-001)) | **Request CAS** enables when both look valid |
| 3 | Click **Request CAS** | Shows **CAS request submitted** and a reference; adds CAS senders to your Gmail monitoring and starts a Gmail sync |
| 4 | Wait 5–60 minutes | The email is imported automatically when Gmail is connected; otherwise use [W-MF-001](W-MF-001.md) |

**Success:** A new CAS import appears under **Recent imports**.
**If something goes wrong**
| Symptom / message | Why | How to recover |
|---|---|---|
| "Too many requests for this operation. Please wait a minute." | More than 5 requests in a minute | Wait |
| "Internal server error" | CASParser not configured or rejected the call | Try [W-MF-002](W-MF-002.md) or upload a PDF |
