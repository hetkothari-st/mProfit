# Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 80C + 24(b) | Income-tax deductions for home loans: principal under 80C (up to ₹1,50,000 overall) and interest under 24(b) (up to ₹2,00,000 for a self-occupied home). |
| 80E | Income-tax deduction for interest on an education loan, uncapped, for up to 8 years. |
| Account Access | Your page listing the accountants and advisors you have let into your books, what each can reach, and everything they have done; you can withdraw access here on any plan. |
| Account Aggregator (Finvu) | RBI-regulated consent framework, via Finvu, for pulling financial data; consents are started on the Connectors page |
| Accounts you manage | User-menu list of family members without their own login whose accounts you keep; choosing one shows "You are managing <name>'s account". |
| accrued | An interest-earning holding valued at principal plus interest earned so far. |
| Add asset class | Sidebar button that adds an optional class (Bonds, Crypto, Forex, NPS, Post Office) to the sidebar. |
| Add my share to Cash Activity | Switch on a split expense that adds your share as an outflow in your Cash Activity and keeps it updated. |
| Advance tax | Tax paid in four instalments during the year (15 June, 15 September, 15 December, 15 March). |
| AI Assistant | The built-in chat ("Portfolio Assistant") opened from the round button on every page; answers need Plus or higher. |
| AI connectors | Settings page where you connect your own ChatGPT or Claude to your EveryPaisa data (read-only, for the areas you choose). |
| AI-use notice | "How EveryPaisa uses AI in advice" — must be accepted before the assistant answers. |
| AIF | Alternative Investment Fund. |
| Alerts & Reminders | The bell in the header and the Alerts page: maturities, premium due dates, expiries and your own reminders. |
| AMFI NAV | The daily net asset value per unit published by AMFI; used to price mutual fund holdings. |
| Amortization schedule | The month-by-month table of EMI, interest, principal and balance, recomputed from the loan's terms and recorded payments. |
| Anchor (Good condition) | The valuation's base price for a vehicle in Good condition, before the condition sliders. |
| Approve & commit | Accept an event so it is written as a transaction or cash-flow entry. |
| APR (true yearly cost) | The yearly rate that equates what you actually receive (loan minus fees) with everything you repay. |
| as of | The date of the price behind a holding's value; shown amber with ⚠ when older than 5 days (2 days for crypto). |
| Asset Classes | The sidebar section listing each kind of asset or liability you track (Stocks, Mutual Funds, FDs & RDs, Loans, …). You can reorder, hide and add classes. |
| At maturity | The projected amount a deposit or scheme pays at its maturity date. |
| Auto-commit | A monitored-sender setting: its confident events are written without manual review. |
| Auto-created folder | A Documents person folder the app created from a name found on a document. |
| Auto-fetch account | An EPF UAN or PPF bank account linked so its passbook can be fetched from the portal. |
| Auto-matched | A rent month marked received automatically from an imported bank credit. |
| Back to my account | Returns from managing a family member's account to your own. |
| Backup code | One of 10 one-time codes (format ABCD-EFGH) shown when two-factor is turned on; each signs you in once |
| Balance due / To collect / In advance | What the tenant owes (positive) or has paid ahead (negative) on the khata. |
| Billing cycle | Monthly (30 days) or Annual (365 days) when buying a plan |
| Break-even | The month in which cumulative EMI savings first cover the switching costs. |
| Broker Connectors | The Connectors page cards that store your Zerodha, Upstox or Angel One developer keys and broker login |
| Bucket | One of seven advisor asset groups: Equity — Domestic, Equity — International, Debt, Gold, Real assets, Cash equivalent, Other / alternatives. |
| By value / My order | Sort choice for the Asset Classes sidebar section: largest holding first, or the order you set with **Edit**. |
| CAGR (goal) | The yearly return needed from today for a goal's current value to reach its target by the target date. |
| Carat / Purity | Gold fineness (24K, 22K, 18K, 14K) or silver grade (999, 925, 800) used to scale the per-gram price. |
| CAS | Consolidated Account Statement — the statement of your mutual fund and demat holdings that you can request from CAMS, KFintech, NSDL or CDSL by email. |
| Cash Activity | The page of bank credits and debits parsed from your connected accounts (sidebar Overview → Cash Activity). |
| Cash flow | A money-in (**Inflow**) or money-out (**Outflow**) entry created from approved bank alerts, rent and split settlements. |
| CASParser | casparser.in, a third-party service the app uses to request a KFintech CAS by PAN + email. |
| CE / PE | Call option / put option. |
| Challan | A traffic e-challan (fine) issued against a vehicle. |
| Chart of accounts | The tree of ledger accounts vouchers post to. |
| CII | Cost Inflation Index used to index a property's cost for long-term capital gain. |
| Client books | Sidebar entry (page title "Clients") for a professional who has been given access to someone else's books. |
| CMP | Current market price of one unit of a holding. |
| CoinGecko | The crypto price source; a coin "linked to CoinGecko" gets live prices. |
| Collections | The list of active tenants who currently owe money. |
| Coming up | The FDs & RDs list of maturities within 30 days and RD installments due within 7 days or overdue. |
| Connector address | The `/mcp` web address you paste into ChatGPT or Claude |
| Contract note | The broker's legally required record of the trades you made on a day. |
| Contributor (family) | Member who sees other members' data within their visibility caps and can add to family-shared portfolios. |
| Cost basis (property) | Purchase price + stamp duty + registration fee + brokerage + other costs. |
| cost basis / at cost | A holding with no market price is valued at what you paid for it. |
| Coverage check | The page that compares your life, health, vehicle and home cover with what your figures suggest you need. |
| Critical illness cover | A lump sum paid on diagnosis of a listed illness, recorded as Yes / No / Not recorded on a policy. |
| Current value | On asset-class pages: today's value of a holding — market price × units, interest accrued to today for deposits and compounding Post Office schemes, cost for payout schemes, and nothing for classes without a price source. |
| Customer ID | The bank-issued customer number (CIF); the same for all your accounts at one bank. |
| Danger zone | The Settings card holding **Delete account** |
| Data checks | The checks above the Reports tabs that say whether figures are complete and current. |
| Demo mode | Server setting under which Account Aggregator consents are test fixtures approved with **Simulate approval** |
| Deposit held | Security deposit received minus deposit refunds; kept outside the rent balance. |
| Difference in Opening Balances | The balancing line added to the balance sheet when opening balances don't net to zero. |
| Direct / Regular plan | Two plans of the same scheme; Regular includes distributor commission and costs more each year. |
| Drift | Current weight minus target weight of a bucket, in percentage points (pp). 5 pp or more is treated as material. |
| Duplicate / Probably fine | Labels in **Find duplicates**: a set that is almost certainly recorded twice (pre-ticked), or a set with a likely reason to be separate (not ticked). |
| Effective funds | 1 ÷ Σ(weight²) across your funds; how many equal-sized funds your book behaves like. |
| Effective plan | The plan in force: a paid plan past its end date counts as Free |
| EMI | Equated monthly instalment — the fixed monthly payment that repays a loan's principal and interest. |
| Engagement | A professional's active relationship with one client; **Close engagement** ends it from the professional's side. |
| Equity Holdings | The Stocks page table aggregating each stock across portfolios. |
| ETF | Exchange-traded fund; shown with stocks, tagged "ETF". |
| Event | A single financial fact read from an email (a buy, a UPI credit, a premium…) waiting for review or already written to your books. |
| EveryPaisa | The product: a multi-asset portfolio ledger for Indian investors ("A multi-asset ledger for India"). |
| Executed (will) | A generated will whose signing has been recorded. |
| Executor | Person who carries out the will. |
| EXPIRY ? | Status of an F&O position waiting for its expiry close to be approved. |
| Expiry close | A request created on expiry day to close an open F&O contract at the exchange settlement price, approved or rejected on the Expiry tab. |
| F & O | Futures and options (the derivatives asset class). |
| F&O | Futures and options contracts, tracked on the Futures & Options page as positions rather than holdings. |
| Family | Paid plan that adds family sharing (3 seats included), consolidated family net worth and reports, on top of Plus. |
| Family insurance sheet | The printable one-page summary of active policies for your family. |
| Family view / Switch view | Header menu that shows the app as a family's combined data instead of your Personal view. |
| FD | Fixed deposit: a one-time deposit with a bank or issuer. |
| FDs & RDs | Fixed deposits and recurring deposits. |
| Financial Health Score | The 0–100 score and A–F grade on the Portfolio Score page. |
| Finding | One rule result with evidence, from an analysis run. |
| Finvu / Finfactor Wealthscape | An account-aggregator provider and its data API used by the sandbox card. |
| FMV (31 Jan 2018) | Fair market value on 31 January 2018, used to grandfather long-term equity gains under Section 55(2)(ac). |
| Foreclosure | Paying off the whole balance early; recording one closes the loan as Foreclosed. |
| Free | The starting plan for every new account: net worth and dashboard, manual entry across all asset classes, 1 portfolio, basic reports. |
| Free preview | The single locked answer a Free account can see in the AI Assistant. |
| Fund CAGR | The fund's annualised NAV growth from your first purchase date to today. |
| Futures Ledger | The futures section of the F&O open/closed tabs. |
| FX pair | A currency-pair position such as USDINR or EURUSD, treated as speculative business income. |
| FY (financial year) | The Indian April–March tax year, written "2026-27". |
| Grace period | The 30 days between asking to delete an account and its permanent erasure; signing in offers **Restore account** |
| Grandfathering | For equity bought on or before 31 January 2018, cost can be raised to that day's fair market value. |
| Grandfathering / FMV | For equity bought on or before 31-Jan-2018, the cost can be raised to its fair market value (closing price) on that date. |
| Guardian | Adult appointed for children under 18. |
| Hand the account to them | Inviting a managed member to take over their profile with their own email and password. |
| Hide values | The eye button in the top bar that covers amounts and numbers across the app with grey bars; remembered on this browser. |
| Hide values / Show values | Header eye button that masks money amounts on screen on this browser. |
| Holding | What you own of one asset in one portfolio, rebuilt by the app from your transactions (units, cost, current value). |
| House methodology | The firm's signed fund-ranking method, scored nightly, used to name funds in recommendations when enabled. |
| Hypothecation | A lender's charge on a vehicle bought on loan; "Clear" means none recorded. |
| I'm OK | Email link that resets the Succession countdown. |
| IFSC | The 11-character bank branch code (for example HDFC0001234). |
| Import job | One uploaded or emailed file being processed; shown as a row in **Import history** with a status. |
| Inbox | Sidebar section for connecting Gmail ("Connect your Gmail") so statements and alerts are imported from your mailbox. |
| Inbox imports | The Reports tab where documents found by an inbox scan are approved or rejected. |
| Inbox scan | A one-off search of a date range of your Gmail for financial attachments to approve and import. |
| Indexation / CII | Raising the cost of an asset by the Cost Inflation Index of the sale year over the purchase year. |
| Ingestion failure | A record kept when a file, row or email could not be processed, with its raw data, for review or automatic retry (the "dead-letter queue"). |
| Installment schedule | The month-by-month list of an RD's (or Post Office RD/SSY's) installments with Mark paid / Undo. |
| Intraday (speculative) | A buy and sell of the same security on the same day; taxed as speculative business income under Sec. 43(5). |
| Invested | On asset-class pages: the cost of the units you still hold, including charges. |
| Khata | The tenant's running ledger: charges ("You gave") and money in ("You got"), with a balance. |
| KVP | Kisan Vikas Patra (Post Office). |
| live / live from Gmail | A bank balance last updated automatically from a bank alert e-mail. |
| Loan given | Money you lent to someone; what they still owe counts as an asset. |
| Loan taken | Money you borrowed from a lender and are repaying; counts as a liability. |
| Locked | A feature your plan does not include. It shows "<feature> is locked" with **View plans**. |
| Look-through | Your exposure to the stocks, sectors and bonds inside your funds, weighted by what you hold. |
| Lot | The contract size of an F&O instrument; quantities are shown as units with "×<lot>". |
| LRS | Liberalised Remittance Scheme: up to USD 250,000 per person per financial year sent abroad. |
| LTP | Last traded price used to value a stock, ETF or F&O contract. |
| Luhn check | The checksum every card number carries; catches a mistyped digit. |
| Mailback | A registrar's "email me my statement" request; the CAS arrives by email locked with a password. |
| Make rental | Creates a linked Rental record from an owned property. |
| Managed member | A family member with no login whose books another member keeps; cannot sign in until they take the account over. |
| Market rate | The lowest current published minimum rate for your loan type among tracked lenders. |
| May have lapsed / Cover has ended | A premium past its grace period (or past its due date with no grace) with no payment recorded. |
| MCLR | Marginal cost of funds based lending rate — a lender's internal benchmark; floating loans here use its 1-year MCLR, reset yearly. |
| MIS | Post Office Monthly Income Scheme. |
| Model portfolio | A set of target weights per asset bucket. Four are created for you (one per risk category) on your first assessment. |
| Monitored sender | An email address (or `@domain`) whose emails the app is allowed to read for financial events. |
| Needs attention | The Documents card holding files that are still being read or need a password, review or assignment. |
| Needs password | Import status: the file is encrypted and none of the automatic passwords opened it. |
| Net after debts | Dashboard figure: Total Net Worth minus outstanding loans and credit-card balances. |
| Net saving | Interest saved by refinancing minus switching costs. |
| Net worth over time | Dashboard chart of your net worth after liabilities, from one snapshot taken each night at 23:45 IST. |
| Next premium due | The date of the earliest unpaid premium; when entered on a new policy, earlier premiums count as already paid ("untracked"). |
| Next rate reset | The date your floating loan's rate is next revised to its benchmark. |
| no price | No current price is available for the holding, so no profit or loss is shown. |
| Nominee (Succession) | A person who receives read-only access to the information you chose after release. |
| Nominee only (will ownership) | An asset paid to its nominee rather than passing under the will; cannot be gifted. |
| Nominees notified | Succession stage in which nominees are told access opens in 7 days. |
| NSC | National Savings Certificate (Post Office). |
| Off-track | A goal whose needed yearly return is higher than the expected return you entered. |
| Ombudsman | The Insurance Ombudsman, to whom an unresolved complaint can be taken (claims up to ₹50 lakh). |
| Options Chain | On the F&O page, the options section listing your CE/PE positions (not an exchange option chain). |
| Outstanding (loan) | The balance still owed, taken as the opening balance of the next unpaid EMI on or after today. |
| Overdue / This week / This month / Later | Alert bands: past due; due in 7 days or fewer; in 30 days or fewer; beyond 30 days. |
| Overlap | Σ min(weight in fund A, weight in fund B) over securities both funds hold. |

| Owner (family) | Family member who manages members and sees every member's data without caps. |

| Pending tenant reminders | Email/SMS rent reminders waiting for the landlord's approval before sending. |

| Pillar | A group of metrics in a fund's score (for example Returns, Consistency, Cost). |
| Plan | Your subscription level: **Free**, **Plus**, **Family** or **Pro/Advisor**. Each plan includes everything in the plans below it; a paid plan lasts 30 days (monthly) or 365 days (annual) from payment and then returns to Free. |
| Planning | Sidebar group with Goals, Will Maker, Succession, Tax Planning and AI connectors. |
| Plus | Paid plan that adds the full tax/CA report catalog, account-aggregator auto-import, more portfolios, AI insights and the AI Assistant, goal projections, and the other Plus features listed on the Pricing page. |
| PMS | Portfolio Management Service. |
